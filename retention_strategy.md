# Retention Strategy

## Champions

### Objective
Maintain loyalty and maximize lifetime value.

### Actions
- Early access to new products
- VIP rewards program
- Personalized recommendations
- Exclusive promotional campaigns

---

## Loyal Customers

### Objective
Increase purchase frequency and prevent migration to competitors.

### Actions
- Loyalty point multipliers
- Product bundle offers
- Personalized email campaigns

---

## Potential Loyalists

### Objective
Convert into loyal customers.

### Actions
- Welcome-back promotions
- Cross-category recommendations
- Targeted discounts on preferred products

---

## At Risk Customers

### Objective
Prevent churn.

### Actions
- Re-engagement campaigns
- Personalized coupons
- Customer satisfaction surveys
- Follow-up communication

---

## Lost Customers

### Objective
Attempt selective win-back campaigns.

### Actions
- Win-back discounts
- Limited-time offers
- Feedback collection campaigns

---

## Priority Recommendation

The At Risk segment contains the largest customer population and should be prioritized for retention investment.
