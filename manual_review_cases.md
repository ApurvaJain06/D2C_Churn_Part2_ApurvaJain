# Manual Review Cases

## Case 1: High Spending Customer With Declining Activity

Characteristics:
- High monetary value
- Low recent engagement
- Falling purchase frequency

Recommended Action:
Assign to customer success team for proactive outreach.

---

## Case 2: Frequent Support Ticket Customer

Characteristics:
- Multiple support interactions
- Reopened tickets
- Negative sentiment indicators

Recommended Action:
Review service issues and provide personalized resolution.

---

## Case 3: High Return Customer

Characteristics:
- Elevated return behavior
- Reduced purchasing activity

Recommended Action:
Investigate product quality or expectation mismatch.

---

## Case 4: Campaign Non-Responsive Customer

Characteristics:
- Received multiple campaigns
- Low engagement despite interventions

Recommended Action:
Evaluate alternative retention strategies.

---

## Case 5: Recently Inactive Loyal Customer

Characteristics:
- Historically strong customer
- Sudden decline in recency score

Recommended Action:
Trigger early warning retention workflow.
